Delete your Augrant account

  1. Open the Augrant app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything linked to it (profile, posts, comments, messages, friend list, Auru balance and items) is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered.

Can't open the app? Open an issue at github.com/Augrixs/Augrant/issues with your username and we will delete the account.
