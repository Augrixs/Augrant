Augrant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Augrant ("the app") is made by Augrixs. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Google account details you sign in with: email address and name. Used only to create and secure your account.
  • Profile details you enter: username, display name, optional birthday and gender, profile picture and cover.
  • Content you create and share: posts, comments, likes, friend requests, chat messages, and information about clips you export (title, length, quality).
  • Usage information: when you open and close the app and actions such as exporting a clip, used for safety and fixing bugs.
  • In-app items: your Auru balance, claims, purchases and equipped cosmetics (virtual items with no real-money value).

Your voice recordings, background videos and projects are processed and stored on your own device. They are not uploaded unless you choose to post them.

How we use it

To run the app and its social features, keep the community safe (automatic text filtering, reports, bans), and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who can see it

Your username, display name, picture, posts and comments are visible to other users. Chat messages are visible to you and the person you message. Birthday, gender and email are not shown to other users. App administrators can view account and moderation information to keep the community safe.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our backend provider Supabase and sign-in is provided by Google. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Retention and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all linked data at any time in the app: Settings, then Delete account. See also how to delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Augrixs/Augrant/issues.
